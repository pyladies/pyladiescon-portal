# Speakers

This guide walks you through the speaker side of the PyLadiesCon portal, from
sending your first proposal to the day of the conference. There are two ways
to become a speaker, and each has its own walkthrough with a screen recording:

- [You send a proposal](#flow-1-you-send-a-proposal), from the public
  "Propose a session" link. If you volunteered with us last year, you already
  have an account and can use it.
- [We invite you](#flow-2-we-invite-you), and you accept the invitation from
  the email we send you.

Whichever way you come in, you end up in the same place: your speaker
dashboard, with a personal checklist that stays with you until the end of the
conference.

!!! note "Speaking, volunteering and everything else use one account"
    There is no separate speaker account. The account you use to volunteer,
    or for anything else in the portal, is the account you use to speak. If
    you have never used the portal, you create an account when you propose,
    or we create one for you when you accept an invitation.

## Why we use the portal

Speakers used to send us their details through forms, emails and shared
spreadsheets. The portal replaces all of that with one place where you can:

- see exactly what we need from you and by when, in your own timezone;
- see what the team is doing for you, so nothing is a mystery;
- keep your bio, photo and session details up to date in one place, instead of
  sending them again every time someone asks;
- get reminders before things are due, from one system, instead of several.

Your journey does not end when you submit your title and abstract. The
checklist keeps going through scheduling, your tech check, promo materials and
the conference itself, so you and the organizers can both see how far along
things are.

## Flow 1: you send a proposal

Use this flow if you found the propose link on our website, in a blog post or
in a message from us. It works the same whether you are brand new or you
volunteered with PyLadiesCon in a previous year.

### Before you start

- Proposals are only open during the edition's proposal window. If the
  "Propose a session" button is missing from the portal home page, we have not
  opened proposals yet.
- You can propose up to **3 sessions** that are waiting for an answer at the
  same time.
- A title and two sentences is enough to start. You can edit your proposal
  afterwards.

### Steps

1. Go to the [portal](https://portal.pyladies.com) and choose **Propose a
   session**, or open
   [portal.pyladies.com/speakers/propose](https://portal.pyladies.com/speakers/propose/)
   directly.
2. If you are not signed in, you land on a page that asks you to sign in.
    - **You volunteered before, or have used the portal for anything else:**
      choose **Sign in to the one I have** and sign in with your username and
      password. If you cannot remember your password, choose **Send me a
      sign-in code** and we will email you a one-time code instead.
    - **You have never used the portal:** choose **Create an account**. You
      will be asked to confirm your email address.
3. After signing in, the proposal form opens. Fill in the **About you** part:
    - **Display name** (required): how your name should appear on the schedule.
    - **Pronouns**, **Location**, and **Timezone** (required). We use your
      timezone to show you times in yours and to know when to reach you.
    - **Bio** (required): a couple of sentences is plenty. Markdown is
      supported.
    - **Headshot**: optional now, and you can add one later.
    - Links to your website, GitHub, Mastodon, LinkedIn and Bluesky, all
      optional.
4. Fill in the **Your session** part:
    - **What kind of session** (required): only the kinds we are currently
      accepting are listed, each with its length and whether it is live or
      pre-recorded.
    - **Title** and **Summary** (required). The summary is what attendees would
      see on the schedule.
    - **Outline**, **Prerequisites** and **Audience** are optional but help us
      plan.
    - **Level** and **Language**. The language is a code such as `en` or
      `pt-br`. Leave the level as "Not sure yet" if you are unsure.
5. Choose **Send the proposal**.
6. You land on **My proposals**, where your proposal shows as **Pending
   review**. From there you can **Edit** it or **Withdraw** it while nobody has
   answered yet. We also email you a confirmation.

!!! tip "Already speaking this year?"
    If you are already on the program, the form skips the **About you** half,
    because we already have those details. Proposing another session is one
    form.

### What happens next

The organizers review proposals and answer each one by email.

- **Approved:** you become a speaker on the program. The **Speaking** menu
  appears in the portal, your checklist is created, and you get a welcome
  email. Carry on with [your speaker dashboard](#your-speaker-dashboard).
- **Not this time:** you get a short note. Slots sometimes open up later, so
  an answered proposal can still be approved afterwards, and you would hear
  from us again.
- **Withdrawn:** if you withdraw, your writing is kept. You can edit it and
  choose **Send it again** to put it back in the queue.

## Flow 2: we invite you

Use this flow if an organizer invited you to speak. You will get an email
whose subject starts with "You're invited to PyLadiesCon", usually with a
personal note from the organizer who invited you.

### Steps

1. Open the invitation email and choose the **Accept or decline the
   invitation** button. If the button does not work, copy the address in the
   email into your browser.
   The link is personal to you and works for 14 days. If it expires, ask the
   organizer who invited you to send it again.
2. The invitation page shows what you are being invited to: the type of
   session, its title, and the organizer's note. Choose **Accept the
   invitation**, or **Decline** if you cannot make it.
   Accepting signs you in and creates your speaker account if you do not have
   one yet, so you do not need a password to start.
3. On the **Welcome** page:
    - check your **username**, **first name** and **last name**, and add your
      **pronouns** if you like;
    - agree to the **Code of Conduct** and the **Terms of Service**, which both
      have to be agreed to before you can use the portal;
    - a **password is optional**. Leave it blank to sign in with an emailed
      code next time, or set one now. You can also do this later under
      **Manage account**.
   Then choose **Continue to my dashboard**.
4. Your [speaker dashboard](#your-speaker-dashboard) opens. Your first jobs
   are to fill in your speaker profile and read your checklist.

You also get a "Welcome aboard" email that repeats your username and how to sign
in again.

### Signing in later

Go to the [portal](https://portal.pyladies.com), choose **Login**, and either
use your password or choose **Send me a sign-in code**. We email you a
one-time code and you are in. There is no separate password to remember unless
you want one.

## Your speaker dashboard

Once you are on the program, the **Speaking** menu opens your speaker area.

- **Dashboard:** your sessions and their status, banners for anything that
  needs attention (such as a missing bio or photo), and how many to-dos are
  open and which one is due next.
- **My speaker checklist:** everything we need from you, and everything we are
  doing for you. See below.
- **My speaker profile:** your display name, bio, headshot, location, timezone
  and links. You can untick **Show my bio, photo and links on the public site**;
  your name still appears on your sessions.
- **My sessions:** your sessions, where you can check and edit the title and
  summary.
- **My proposals:** proposals you sent and where they stand.
- **Speaker guide:** the guide for your kind of session. Open it and tick
  **I have read the guide** when you have.
- **Schedule:** your slot, once we have scheduled you, shown in your timezone.

### Your checklist

The checklist has two lists.

**Your to-dos** are the things we need from you, each with a due date shown in
your timezone. Typical items are:

- check your session title and summary;
- update your bio and headshot;
- read the speaker guide;
- join the PyLadiesCon Discord;
- confirm your scheduled slot;
- share a link to your slides;
- register for the conference (it is free, but you still need a ticket);
- do a tech check with the team.

**What we're preparing for you** is the team's side: onboarding email, Discord
channel and role, promo materials, scheduling, the schedule confirmation, and
day-of reminders. You see who is on each item and when it is due, so you can
tell what is waiting on us.

A few labels you will see:

| Label | What it means |
|---|---|
| **ticks itself** | The portal ticks it when you do the thing, for example when your bio and headshot are saved. |
| **Waiting** | This item cannot start yet. The reason is shown below it, for example "your slot is not scheduled yet". It opens by itself when the reason goes away. |
| Due date | Shown in your timezone. Items with no date have no deadline yet. |

Use **All, by due date** or **By session** at the top to switch views if you
have more than one session.

We send you one reminder email when items are due within 7, 3 and 1 days, so
you do not have to keep the checklist open.

## Your information

- The portal asks for what we need to run your session: your name, email,
  bio, optional photo, timezone and links, and your session details.
- Your bio, photo and links are only shown on the public site if you leave
  **Show my bio, photo and links on the public site** ticked.
- The portal does not use advertising or analytics trackers, and we do not
  share your details with third-party analytics services.
- Video uploads for pre-recorded sessions, such as PyJam performances, are not
  open yet. We will email everyone who needs to upload when they are, and the
  upload item on your checklist will open then.

## Getting help

Reply to any email from the portal, or ask your liaison, the organizer looking
after you, if you have one. For accessibility needs, timezone problems or
anything else that does not fit a form, write to us and we will sort it out.
